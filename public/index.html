The important bits

About your space

Designed to be useful on its own, with your data staying under your control.

Where your data lives

Daily records are stored as Markdown in the server's vault folder. The app does not need a cloud account, analytics service or AI connection.

The brain map and insights are generated from the records in that vault. They do not send your journal to an external service.

Privacy and deployment

This version does not include built-in user authentication. Keep it on a trusted private network or behind an authenticated access layer. Do not expose it directly to the public internet.

Petrio and Alfie integration is not enabled. Any future connection will be opt-in and separately scoped.

Looking after your archive

Download a full vault backup regularly. Keep a second copy somewhere separate from the running container. Restoring means placing the backed-up vault in the configured data directory.
